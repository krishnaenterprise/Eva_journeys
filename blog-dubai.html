7 Things You Must Do When Visiting Dubai in 2026

Travel Expert

Dubai is a city of superlatives—home to the tallest building, the largest mall, and the most luxurious hotels on the planet. As we move into 2026, the city has only continued to innovate. If you're planning a trip to this glittering oasis in the UAE, here are 7 absolutely essential experiences you cannot miss.

1. At The Top, Burj Khalifa

No trip to Dubai is complete without visiting the tallest building in the world. Soaring 828 meters into the sky, the Burj Khalifa offers unparalleled 360-degree views of the entire emirate. We recommend booking tickets for sunset to watch the city transition from day to a glittering nightscape.

2. The Museum of the Future

Often dubbed "the most beautiful building on earth," this architectural marvel is entirely devoid of support columns. Inside, you'll find highly interactive, immersive exhibits that explore space travel, climate change, and wellness in the year 2071.

3. Premium Desert Safari

Leave the ultra-modern city behind and venture into the rolling red dunes of the Arabian desert. A premium safari includes thrilling dune bashing in a 4x4, camel rides, sandboarding, and a traditional Bedouin-style camp dinner under the stars complete with fire shows and belly dancing.

4. Shop & Dine at The Dubai Mall

Calling it a "mall" is an understatement. The Dubai Mall features over 1,200 retail outlets, an Olympic-sized ice rink, a massive indoor waterfall, and the incredible Dubai Aquarium & Underwater Zoo. Step outside in the evening to witness the choreographed Dubai Fountain show.

5. Explore Palm Jumeirah

The iconic tree-shaped artificial archipelago is a marvel of modern engineering. Take the monorail down the "trunk" of the palm, relax at ultra-luxurious beach clubs, or visit Atlantis The Palm for world-class dining and the massive Aquaventure Waterpark.

6. Walk Through Old Dubai (Al Fahidi)

For a taste of the city before the oil boom, wander through the winding alleys of the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood. Cross the Dubai Creek on a traditional wooden "abra" boat for just 1 Dirham, and lose yourself in the aromatic Spice Souk and the glittering Gold Souk.

7. Marina Dhow Cruise

Experience the stunning skyline of Dubai Marina from the water. Board a traditional wooden dhow boat in the evening for a relaxing cruise through the illuminated canals, enjoying a premium buffet dinner as the luxury yachts and skyscrapers drift by.
